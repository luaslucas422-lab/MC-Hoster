Free · Windows 10 & 11

Your own Minecraft server, on your own PC.

Pick a version, press Finish, press Start. MC Hoster downloads everything, opens your router, adds anti-cheat and gives you the address for your friends.

Download MC Hoster Update logs

No Java or setup needed: MC Hoster gets what it needs.

Everything a home server needs

Set up in minutes, then keep it running.

Every server type

Paper, Purpur, Pufferfish, Spigot, Fabric and Bedrock, from 1.20.6 to the newest version.

Gh0st AC anti-cheat

On by default. Fly, speed, reach, aura and more are caught and punished.

Friends can join

Opens your router ports itself, finds your IP, and can point your own domain at the server.

Plugins your way

Ready presets, search of Modrinth, Hangar and CurseForge, and folders for your own plugins that update the server when you replace a file.

Looks after your PC

Auto restart, scheduled starts, backups, honest RAM numbers and a storage limit that stops the server before your drive fills up.

Community

Share your server, see what others host, send feedback, and read the update logs right in the app.

Three steps

From download to playing.

  1. Open MC Hoster

    Accept the terms, enter your name and Minecraft username. You're made operator on your servers.

  2. Pick and finish

    Server type, version, name and max players. Press Finish and MC Hoster sets it all up.

  3. Start and share

    Press Start. When it says online, copy the address from the Play tab and send it to your friends.

Questions

Good to know.

Is it free?

Yes. MC Hoster is free.

Can I use my own plugins?

Yes. Put them in your plugins folder, tick them in tab 5, and the server gets new versions when you replace the file.

Windows says it's unknown software.

MC Hoster isn't signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. Only download it from this page or GitHub.

Is it made by Mojang?

No. It's an independent project and isn't connected to Mojang or Microsoft.
