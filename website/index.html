Questions

Good to know.

Is it free?

Yes, MC Hoster is free, and everything you need to run a server stays free.

Is there a Premium version?

Yes, if you want it: €5 once, for life. Nothing that's free was taken away. Premium adds extras: up to 3000 players (free: 1500), 20 backups every 6 hours with a copy to a second folder like a USB stick or OneDrive, Discord alerts when your server goes online, offline or crashes and when players join, a player history (who played, when, how long), the Midnight and Ocean themes and your own background picture. See your key any time on My license.

Windows says it's unknown software. Is it safe?

MC Hoster isn't signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows warns about it. Click More info, then Run anyway. Only download it from this page or the GitHub releases.

Can strangers find my IP?

Anyone you give the address to can see your IP. Only share it with people you trust, use the whitelist, or point a domain at it. MC Hoster never shows your IP in the Hosted servers list.

Are plugins and mods safe?

Most are, but some are made to harm you, for example by making their maker operator. Only download from official sites like Modrinth, Hangar and CurseForge, and from people you trust.

Can I use my own plugins?

Yes. Put them in your plugins folder (Documents\MC Hoster Plugins), tick them in tab 5, and click Apply. Replace a file and the server gets the new one on its next start.

What if my world breaks?

Open the Play tab, click Backups, pick one and click Restore (with the server stopped). The world as it was is kept too, just in case.

Is it made by Mojang?

No. MC Hoster is an independent project. It is not an official Minecraft product and is not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft.
