Privacy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

MC Hoster runs on your own computer. Most of what it does never leaves it. This page explains what does.

What stays on your computer

Your servers, worlds, backups, settings, console logs, plugin folders and the name and Minecraft username you enter at first start are saved on your computer only, unless you use an online feature below.

What MC Hoster sends, and when

  • Send feedback: your message, the type you chose, your in-app name, your Minecraft username, the MC Hoster version, any photos you add, and the console lines if you tick that box (IP addresses in them are hidden first). Feedback is not private: other people can read it.
  • Server sharing: your posts, with your in-app name and Minecraft username.
  • Hosted servers: only if you click "List my server here": the server's name, type, version, max players, icon, and your names. Never its IP address. Listings disappear after 3 days unless MC Hoster is opened again.
  • Downloads and updates: MC Hoster contacts GitHub, Mojang, PaperMC, FabricMC, Adoptium, Modrinth, Hangar, CurseForge and similar sites to download software and check for updates. Like any website, they see your IP address and have their own privacy policies.
  • Your IP and router: to show the address your friends join with and to open ports, MC Hoster asks a public IP service and your router (UPnP). This information stays in the app.

This website

This website has no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies. It is hosted by Render, loads fonts from Google Fonts and pictures and update logs from GitHub. Those services can see your IP address when your browser contacts them.

Who can see it, and for how long

The online features run on a server hosted by Render. Data is kept until it is deleted or the feature stops. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what you sent, or object to it being used. Ask through Send feedback in MC Hoster or an issue on GitHub (don't put private details in a public issue). If you live in the EU or UK you can also complain to your data protection authority; in Ireland that is the Data Protection Commission.

Children

If you are under 16 (or the age of digital consent where you live), ask a parent or guardian before using Send feedback, Server sharing or Hosted servers.
